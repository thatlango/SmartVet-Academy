Layer production

Build the routines behind consistent egg production.

A practical pathway for poultry farmers learning how to manage layer birds from brooding through laying, with attention to feed, health, records and business performance.

What you should understand

  • Brooding and pullet management.
  • Feeding and water requirements through production stages.
  • Lighting and laying management.
  • Biosecurity, vaccination and flock-health observation.
  • Egg handling, records and production monitoring.
  • Costs, revenues and farm-management decisions.

Who this pathway is for

Layer farmers, poultry workers, agricultural trainees and entrepreneurs building or improving egg-production enterprises.
