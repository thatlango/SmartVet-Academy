Dual-purpose poultry

Learn practical Kroiler poultry management.

A learning pathway for farmers using dual-purpose birds for meat, eggs or mixed household and commercial production.

What you should understand

  • Preparing housing and receiving chicks.
  • Feeding and water management across growth stages.
  • Health, biosecurity and mortality monitoring.
  • Growth, egg production and flock records.
  • Managing dual-purpose production objectives.
  • Costs, sales and practical farm business decisions.

Who this pathway is for

Smallholder and commercial farmers, farm workers and agricultural learners interested in resilient dual-purpose poultry systems.
