Broiler production

Learn broiler poultry farming from preparation to market.

A practical learning pathway for farmers and farm staff who want to manage broiler birds with stronger production discipline, better records and clearer business decisions.

What you should understand

  • Preparing the poultry house before chicks arrive.
  • Brooding, temperature and early flock management.
  • Feeding, water and growth monitoring.
  • Biosecurity, common health risks and mortality records.
  • Housing, stocking and daily routines.
  • Production records, costs, sales and flock performance.

Who this pathway is for

New and growing poultry farmers, farm workers, youth entering poultry production and programme participants who need practical broiler-management skills.
